Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c) competition "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head".

`main.py` writes one short office joke — a single line with a setup and a
turn, punchline last — to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network,
no input files.

> I suggested replacing our weekly status meeting with an email — now we hold a weekly meeting to discuss the email.
